## Launch-inlägget (fäst det)

~1 480 tecken · vecket efter "…BTC is sitting near a four-month low, and" · bifoga `grafik/launch.png`. Kolla att BTC fortfarande ligger nära fyramånaderslägsta innan du postar, annars stryk den meningen.

```text
$HELD is live.

Every dip has a seller. HELD checks who.

When a Solana coin drops 30% in an hour, it reads who sold. Top 10 holders still in: it buys the dip. Whales dumping: it skips.

whalesheld.fun

Right now everything dips. BTC is sitting near a four-month low, and every chart on Solana looks the same: a red candle and a timeline asking who's selling.

The candle can't tell you. Panic from small wallets and a whale walking out look identical on a chart. One is a discount. The other is an exit.

What's live on the site:

→ The board: every Solana coin dipping right now, marked HELD, SOLD or WATCH, with the reason in one line
→ Dip X-ray: paste any CA and see the top 10 bags before and after the dip, and what paper hands dumped in SOL
→ Share card: every X-ray turns into a card you can post
→ The log: every call saved with its price and scored 1 hour and 24 hours later, wins and misses

How it reads a dip: the last hour of trades from GeckoTerminal, matched against the top holders from Solana RPC. Whale bags that moved without a trade get checked on-chain. No API keys, so anyone can check the work.

The engine runs on creator fees. Every 1 SOL: 0.60 buys the dips the whales held, 0.30 buys back and burns $HELD, 0.10 runs the data. Every profit goes back into $HELD. It's in beta: buys run by hand from one public wallet, every trade on-chain.

Try it: paste the coin you're down on and see who sold it to you.

Buys the dip whales didn't sell.
```
